01 / The Project

Built for
the community.

JUP9IW is a personal/community development project focused on experimentation, preservation and understanding of the MWIII-era experience.

JUP9IW is being developed as a community project for people interested in Call of Duty, game preservation and custom development.


The goal is to create a modern and organized platform where project updates, development information and community resources can be shared in one place.


Development is an ongoing process. Features, compatibility and project information may change as the project evolves.
